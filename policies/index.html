BrixBlox Policies

BrixBlox is a friendly place for players of every age. These rules keep it that way. Please read them before you build, chat or play.

GamesItemsThreatsLanguageWarnings & bans

Inappropriate parts in games

BrixBlox does not allow any sort of inappropriate blocks or builds in a game, like sexual images or rigs doing inappropriate actions. This should be clear at all times because this is a friendly platform for any player.

  • No sexual, graphic or disturbing builds, signs, avatars or animations.
  • No games that exist only to shock, scare or harass other players.
  • You are responsible for everything inside the games you publish.

Offensive items

BrixBlox does not allow anything offensive to our player base. Please refrain from using any bad or discriminatory language, in item names, descriptions, textures or anything else you make.

  • No hateful or discriminatory symbols, names or text.
  • No items that copy or mock a real person in a harmful way.

Threats

BrixBlox is a place where everyone feels safe. You shouldn’t be making anyone feel unsafe.

  • No threats of any kind, in chat, game text, usernames or anywhere else.
  • No bullying, stalking or targeting a player over and over.

Do not threaten people with bad language. This has a chance of becoming a permanent ban.

Bad language

Bypassing the BrixBlox filtration system is not allowed. Please refrain from doing this again.

  • Do not swap letters, add spaces or use symbols to get a blocked word past the filter.
  • Chat that is filtered may be logged and reviewed by moderators.

Warnings and bans

If you break a rule, a moderator can send you a warning. A warning shows the reason and what to change. If you keep breaking the rules, your account can be banned. A ban can last for a set time or be permanent, depending on what happened.

  • Serious or repeated breaks can lead to a ban without further warning.
  • Games or items that break these policies can be removed.
  • Think a mistake was made? Ask the BrixBlox team respectfully, and do not make a second account to get around a ban.
These policies can change over time. Playing BrixBlox means you agree to follow them.
