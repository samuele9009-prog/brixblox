Hold right mouse and drag to look. Click and drag a part or model to move it around the map (press R while dragging to turn it 90 degrees, T to tilt it). W A S D to fly, Q down, E up, scroll to zoom. Click a part for move arrows, press R to switch tools (move, resize, rotate; Shift+R goes back). Rotate: drag a ring, hold Shift to snap. Right-click and drag a bottom corner of the stage to resize it. Press Play to walk around with WASD and Space.


      

        

        
Weld: select the Torso first, then ctrl-click the parts to weld to it.

        
P adds a part in front of you · Ctrl+G groups into a model · Ctrl+Shift+G unions the selection into one single part (Ctrl+Alt+U splits it back) · Ctrl+Alt+G ungroups · Backspace deletes · Alt+click (or double-click) a piece of a model to select just that piece. · L locks or unlocks the selection. · Arrow keys nudge the selection by the snap size, PageUp/PageDown move it up and down, End drops it onto whatever is under it, Ctrl+A selects everything.
