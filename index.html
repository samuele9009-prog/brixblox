How you earn

  • 1 blockbuck for every new player who plays your game.
  • 2 blockbucks for every hour you spend playing games.
  • 30 blockbucks every time a Premium player spends an hour in your game (added to your wallet).
  • 100% of the price every time a player buys one of your gamepasses (added to your wallet).
  • 100 plays of your game in one hour: 2 blockbucks for every play that hour (so 200 for 100 plays, 2,000 for 1,000 plays).
